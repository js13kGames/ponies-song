<!-- This project can be considered as a fandom project to My Little Pony -->

Magic is something dark. This statement is true to the lower ponies in this world line.

As the Unicorns rule with their magic, normal ponies without magic are considered as "useless". The normal ponies are put into heavy labors to work for the Unicorns.

They have no way to change their life.

Wait, don't they?!!

--------------------------------

Drag the ponies to squares, when you are ready, click the "End Turn" button to start a round, beat all the enemies to pass the level.
Click them to get them off the battle field if you have at least one Command Point.
For touch screens, you may want to hold the icon for a little bit longer to drag (as like you are dragging a image from a normal webpage.)

Supply Points: Points needed to upgrade your ponies.
Command Points: Points needed to move your ponies during the battle, the remaining Command Points in the end on one round will become Supply Points, so try to save more of them.

---------------------------------

You can play it with a Mouse(Not necessiarily yours) or Finger (Must be yours).

Have Fun!!
